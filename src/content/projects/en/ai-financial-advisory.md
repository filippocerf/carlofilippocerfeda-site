## What it is

A contribution titled *"Artificial Intelligence in Financial Advisory: Towards a Hybrid Advisory Model Between Operational Efficiency and Investor Protection"*, published on the FIA – Financial Insight Assoreti platform.

The work was recognized at the FIA Awards 2026 at Il Salone del Risparmio in Milan, with the Assoreti scholarship and a place in the FIA Talent 2026 programme. I also discussed it in the first episode of the FIA Talks 2026 podcast, hosted by Gianfranco Ursino (Plus24 – Il Sole 24 Ore).

## The idea

Artificial intelligence is by now a general purpose technology that has shifted its centre of gravity from the back office to the front office: no longer only fraud detection, compliance and risk management, but advisory to the client itself. More than 90% of intermediaries already use it, which makes it a competitive necessity rather than an option.

The thesis of the contribution is that the best answer is not full automation, but a hybrid advisory model: the machine processes the data and the calculations, while the advisor keeps responsibility for the decision and the relationship with the client. This is the human-in-the-loop principle, which Article 14 of the AI Act (EU Reg. 2024/1689) turns into a duty of effective human oversight, alongside transparency toward the client and the intermediary's liability regime already outlined by MiFID II and Article 31 of the Italian Consolidated Financial Code.

The critical knot is the opacity of the algorithm: it makes it hard to prove the link between a system malfunction and the harm suffered by the investor. To untie it I propose three converging directions: joint liability across the entire value chain, from the algorithm's designers to the intermediaries, under Article 2055 of the Italian Civil Code; transparency by design that documents every decision step and allows its reconstruction in litigation; and an organized risk management placed on the intermediaries, called to answer for inadequate monitoring of the systems.

## Why it matters

The advisor is not the executor of an algorithm, but the ultimate fiduciary guarantor between technological efficiency and investor protection. It is the reasoning I bring to my daily work: to use AI where it creates real value, without ever losing sight of the regulatory framework and the protection of the person on the other side.
