## What it is

In Linklaters' Innovation team I design and build digital operational workflows to optimize the firm's internal processes and legal delivery.

## What I do

I use Legora for advanced contract automation and for creating dynamic, scalable templates; alongside this, I draft technical summaries, tabular reviews and process observations, so as to coordinate workflows and knowledge-sharing across cross-functional legal teams. The goal is not to take work away from the jurist, but to ease the repetitive part and give back the time that matters.

## The outcome

Faster, repeatable processes with immediate operational value for the teams that use them every day.
