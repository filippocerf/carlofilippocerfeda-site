For years I left traces of what I do across different platforms: a comment here, a project there, a talk that slips under a thousand others within days. None of those places was truly mine, and none preserved the thread of the reasoning.

This site exists to gather that thread in one place: the projects I work on, the thoughts I carry about artificial intelligence and how it is changing work, my commentary on the articles and research I read. The value often lies not in the news itself but in what one makes of it.

I am not trying to convince anyone: I want to leave a readable trace of how I reason, not only of what I have done. If you find something interesting on this site, do not hesitate to write to me.
